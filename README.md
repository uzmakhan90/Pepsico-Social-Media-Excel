## 📘 Project Overview
As a Data Analyst at PepsiCo, I analyzed social media performance data from Instagram, Twitter, Facebook, and YouTube.  
The objective was to create a **Social Media Intelligence Dashboard** in Excel that consolidates campaign data, engagement performance, and content strategy insights to support marketing decisions.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Identify **top-performing content** and **hashtags**.  
- Measure **platform engagement**, **ad spend efficiency**, and **campaign ROI**.  
- Provide **data-driven recommendations** for brand growth and audience loyalty.

---

## 🧩 Tasks Performed
1. **Data Cleaning & Preparation** – Removed duplicates, standardized dates, formatted metrics.  
2. **Engagement Analysis** – Calculated engagement rate and top-performing posts.  
3. **Platform Performance** – Compared engagement vs. ad spend across platforms.  
4. **Hashtag Strategy** – Identified high-impact hashtags and top-performing content types.  
5. **Campaign Effectiveness** – Measured impressions, clicks, and ROI uplift per campaign.  
6. **Follower Retention** – Visualized follower growth and correlation with ad spend.

---

## 💡 Key Insights
- **Instagram** achieved the highest engagement per post.  
- **Video content** drove more clicks and shares than static images.  
- **#PepsiChallenge** and **#GlowHaven** were the top-performing hashtags.  
- Campaigns with steady ad spend showed stronger retention and follower growth.
